About SEVENTEEN

SEVENTEEN

This page is all about the K-pop boy group SEVENTEEN. Here you can learn about their journey, music, personalities, and bond with CARATs.

Their Journey as Trainees

Before becoming SEVENTEEN, the members started as trainees who worked hard to achieve their dreams. They spent years practicing singing, dancing, and performing together.

They faced different challenges during their trainee days, but they continued to work hard and support one another.

On May 26, 2015, SEVENTEEN officially debuted with their first mini album, 17 CARAT, and the song Adore U.

Their Iconic Looks

SEVENTEEN is known for their different concepts and fashion in every era. Their looks can be seen in their music videos, performances, and comebacks.

  • Adore U Era - Fresh and youthful style
  • Mansae Era - Playful and colorful outfits
  • Don't Wanna Cry Era - Elegant styling
  • HOT Era - Bold and powerful looks
  • Super Era - Strong performance outfits

Sweet and Savage Moments

One of the reasons why fans love SEVENTEEN is because of their personalities. They can be sweet and caring, but they can also be funny and chaotic.

Their teasing, jokes, and funny reactions are some of the reasons why fans enjoy watching their variety shows and group content.

Unforgettable Songs

SEVENTEEN has released many songs that CARATs love. Some of their unforgettable songs are:

  • Adore U
  • Pretty U
  • Very Nice
  • Don't Wanna Cry
  • HOT
  • Super

SEVENTEEN and CARATs

CARAT is the official fandom name of SEVENTEEN. The members and CARATs share a special bond through music, concerts, fan meetings, and many memories.

SEVENTEEN always shows appreciation for their fans who continue to support them. Their bond is one of the things that makes being a CARAT special.

13 Members, 3 Units, 1 Team

SEVENTEEN is made up of 13 members who work together as one team. They are known for their teamwork, talents, and dedication to music and performance.
